About BrightPath

Understanding the whole child.

Every student deserves to be understood, supported, and empowered to reach their potential.

Our Mission

To illuminate pathways for student success by helping schools, families, and educational teams understand how students learn, communicate, and engage in their learning environments.

Our Vision

To connect assessment findings to actionable strategies and meaningful educational outcomes through thoughtful collaboration.

Meet the Founder

Dominique Lanier, M.S.Ed.

Certified School Psychologist

Dominique has 8 years of experience supporting students, families, and school teams. Her relationship-centered approach recognizes the unique strengths, experiences, and needs behind every set of assessment results.

Her experience includes learning needs, patterns of strengths and weaknesses, Autism, ADHD, and behavioral concerns. She translates assessment and behavioral information into clear, practical action plans.
